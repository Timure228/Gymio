# Welcome to Gymio!

**Gymio** is a digital fitness and training plan app that allows users to create, structure, and manage their own custom workouts and training programs. An integrated timer assists with completing sets and rest periods during workouts. 

**Target Audience:** Fitness enthusiasts, "gym bros," strength athletes, and individuals who want to independently manage their progress and training routines. 

**Key Features:**
* Execute training programs – perform workout sessions (e.g., Push/Pull/Legs, Upper Body) with guided set and rest intervals.
* Manage training programs – create, edit, and customize personal training plans. 

* Track training days – log completed sessions, exercises, sets, and repetitions to monitor progress.
